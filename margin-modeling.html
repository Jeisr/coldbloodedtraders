Cross vs. Isolated Margin Modeling

Published: August 2026 • 9 min read • By ColdBloodedTraders

In high-volatility crypto environments, the choice between Cross and Isolated margin is not merely an interface setting—it is a fundamental component of your portfolio's risk architecture.

MetricIsolated MarginCross Margin

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the primary difference?

Isolated margin confines risk to the specific capital allocated to a single trade, whereas cross margin allows the entire account equity to be used.
