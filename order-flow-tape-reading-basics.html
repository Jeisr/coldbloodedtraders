MARKET MICROSTRUCTURE Core

Order Flow & Tape Reading Basics

Published: August 2026 • 8 min read • By ColdBloodedTraders

Traditional charting tools display historical price action as candles, lagging behind the real-time battle between buyers and sellers. To capture true market mechanics, systematic traders analyze order flow and read the tape to measure execution velocity, passive depth absorption, and institutional conviction.

"Passive liquidity hides intent, but aggressive market orders reveal true conviction. When large orders repeatedly drain walls without moving price, institutional absorption is underway."

1. Passive vs. Aggressive Execution Mechanics

Understanding order flow requires distinguishing between the two primary forces operating inside the central limit order book:

  • Passive Orders (Limit Orders): Rest on the book, providing structural depth and serving as defense walls or breakout resistance.
  • Aggressive Orders (Market Orders): Cross the spread instantly to consume available liquidity, directly forcing price movement and establishing directional momentum.

2. Tape Reading & Execution Velocity

The "tape" (time-and-sales feed) streams individual transactions in real time. Analyzing print frequency, trade sizes, and Cumulative Delta (CVD) provides critical clues on hidden market sentiment before breakout or reversal validation.

Reaction to Support/Resistance Chases breakouts emotionally Absorbs passive walls systematically

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between passive and aggressive orders in order flow?

Passive orders (limit orders) sit on the book and provide liquidity, acting as structural walls. Aggressive orders (market orders) cross the spread immediately, consuming liquidity and directly driving price action.

How does cumulative delta (CVD) help in tape reading?

Cumulative Delta tracks the running net balance of aggressive buying versus aggressive selling volume. Divergences between price action and CVD often signal impending trend reversals or absorption.
