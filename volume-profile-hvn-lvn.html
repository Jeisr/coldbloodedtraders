VOLUME PROFILE ANALYSIS

Analyzing Volume Profile: Mastering HVN & LVN Zones

Published: August 2026 • Core Module

Volume Profile bridges the gap between time and capital commitment. By mapping traded volume against price levels, traders can identify where institutions have established fair value versus where they have abandoned territory.

"Price is what you pay, but volume profile reveals where market operators established fair value."

1. High Volume Nodes (HVN)

HVNs are zones of consensus. Here, buyers and sellers agree on value, leading to heavy transactional activity. These are the "battlegrounds" where institutional inventory is built.

2. Low Volume Nodes (LVN)

LVNs are zones of rejection. These represent structural voids where price moves rapidly due to a lack of liquidity. These are often the "paths of least resistance" for momentum traders.

Behavior Rotation & Consensus Rejection & Imbalance

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the difference between HVN and LVN?

HVNs are high-activity "fair value" zones; LVNs are low-activity "rejection" corridors.
