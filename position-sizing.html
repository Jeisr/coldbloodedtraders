Position Sizing in Volatile Perpetual Markets

Published: August 2026 • 5 min read • By ColdBloodedTraders

In leverage and perpetual trading, survival precedes profitability. Most retail traders focus heavily on entry signals while completely neglecting the mathematical foundation of position sizing.

"Risking a fixed dollar amount without accounting for volatility and stop-loss distance is a guaranteed path to catastrophic drawdown."

1. The Fixed Percentage Risk Model

Never risk more than 1% to 2% of your total portfolio equity on a single trade setup. Your position size must be calculated based on your stop-loss distance, not an arbitrary leverage multiplier.

2. Mathematical Position Size Formula

To calculate your exact contracts or units to buy/sell:

Position Size = (Account Equity × Risk %) / (Entry Price - Stop Loss Price)

3. Separating Leverage from Risk

Leverage is merely a capital efficiency tool, not a risk parameter. 10x leverage with a 1% stop loss carries the exact same capital risk as 1x leverage with a 1% stop loss if your position size is calibrated correctly.
