LIQUIDITY

Identifying Order Flow & Liquidity Pools

Published: August 2026 • By ColdBloodedTraders

In electronic financial markets, price does not move randomly; it moves toward liquidity. To execute large position sizes, institutional participants require counterparties—meaning large clusters of resting buy or sell orders.

Before executing trades at identified liquidity levels, ensure your risk per trade is strictly defined using our Position Sizing Framework.

2. Where Liquidity Pools Accumulate

Liquidity forms predictably around key technical price levels:

  • Equal Highs/Lows: Retail stop-losses accumulate here.
  • Session Ranges: PDH/PDL and Asian session boundaries.
  • Psychological Figures: Major round numbers attracting retail.

3. Anatomy of a Liquidity Sweep (SFP)

A liquidity grab (Swing Failure Pattern) involves:

  1. Setup: Retail stacks stops at a support/resistance pivot.
  2. Sweep: Price aggressively pierces the level to fill institutional limit orders.
  3. Rejection: Price closes back inside the range, invalidating the breakout.
